### Research Objectives

As quantum hardware advances through the Noisy Intermediate-Scale Quantum (NISQ) era, combining classical computational resilience with quantum Hilbert-space state representation offers profound opportunities for solving high-dimensional combinatorial and non-convex optimization problems.

- **Parameterized Quantum Circuits (PQCs)**: Engineering shallow-depth ansatz designs resilient to barren plateau phenomena and gate noise.
- **Variational Quantum Classifiers (VQCs)**: Formulating variational quantum algorithms in `PennyLane` and `Qiskit` to benchmark quantum state encoding schemes against classical kernel methods.
- **Constrained Optimization**: Evaluating hybrid quantum-classical optimizers (COBYLA, SPSA, and Adam) on multi-constraint financial and network topology graphs.
